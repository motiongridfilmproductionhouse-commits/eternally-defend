# Eterna authority-led public website

## Goal
Reframe the existing premium visual identity around verifiable operating proof: a working protection platform, real aggregate activity, governed processes, and clear company information—without publishing unapproved legal identity, client names, invented testimonials, or unsupported removal outcomes.

## Homepage
- Replace the campaign-style hero copy with operational positioning: digital identity protection for people and organizations in the public eye.
- Use **Request Protection** as the primary action and **Explore the Platform** as the secondary action.
- Identify the brand as **Eterna Sentinel** only. Do not publish “Eterna Sentinel Defence LLC” or an Abu Dhabi headquarters claim until confirmed.
- Add a proof strip near the top using verified aggregate activity, clearly labelled as platform operating activity and dated. Use conservative rounded figures rather than inflated totals.
- Add an anonymous engagement section using factual categories only; no client names, photographs, logos, quotes, or implied endorsements.
- Replace the existing invented anonymous testimonials with a restrained “Protected engagements” presentation that contains no fabricated quotations.
- Expand the platform section with redacted views representing the working system: threat overview, detected assets, investigations, evidence records, enforcement status, monitoring history, and risk assessment. Caption them as redacted platform interfaces.
- Expand “How Eterna operates” into six concrete stages: Verify, Monitor, Investigate, Preserve Evidence, Enforce, Monitor Again.
- Add “Protection in practice” using verified activity only. Describe detected signals, evidence preservation, opened investigations, and submitted actions; do not claim removals or completed outcomes that records do not support.
- Add an Eterna Operations section covering the managed-service model, client categories, protection areas, and brand contact paths.
- Keep the existing governance section and link it to the new Security & Governance page.
- Reorder the homepage to: authority hero, operating proof, protected engagements, protection areas, real platform, operating process, verified activity cases, audiences, governance, FAQ, operations, request protection, corporate footer.

## Navigation and shared site structure
- Change navigation to: Platform, Solutions, How It Works, Case Studies, Company, Resources.
- Change right-side actions to **Client Sign In** and **Request Protection**.
- Build reusable public header, logo, and corporate footer components so new pages remain consistent.
- Footer groups:
  - Platform: Protection, How It Works, Case Studies
  - Company: About, Contact, Leadership, Careers
  - Legal: Privacy Policy, Terms of Service, Cookie Policy, Acceptable Use
  - Client Access: Sign In
- Use **© 2026 Eterna Sentinel. All rights reserved.** while legal entity confirmation is withheld.

## New public pages
- `/about`: who Eterna is, why it exists, the problem it solves, operating approach, technology-assisted review, human judgment, concise Leadership and Careers sections, and a clear note that company details are available through direct enquiry.
- `/security`: authorization, identity verification, access control, evidence preservation, human review, audit history, retention principles, sensitive-information handling, incident reporting, and third-party provider governance. Claims will remain limited to controls substantiated by the product.
- `/case-studies`: anonymized, evidence-led operating examples based only on verified activity states, explicitly avoiding claimed removal outcomes.
- `/terms`, `/cookies`, and `/acceptable-use`: concise owner-authored policy pages consistent with the existing privacy page and current service behavior.
- Do not create an app-owned `/trust`; Lovable reserves the canonical Trust Center route. The public site will link to `/security` instead.
